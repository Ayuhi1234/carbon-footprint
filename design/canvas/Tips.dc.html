YOUR BIGGEST OPPORTUNITY

Transport

Small changes to how you get around can make the biggest difference for you.

Try the metro or bus twice a week
Even a couple of days a week adds up over a month.
Share your ride
Carpool with a colleague or classmate going the same way.
Walk or cycle short trips
For errands under 2 km, leave the bike at home.
