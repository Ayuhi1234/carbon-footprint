How we calculate your footprint

You answer in your own words
We never ask for exact numbers.
AI reads your answers
It picks out the key details — like distance, fuel type or how often.
Fixed formulas do the maths
Those details go through fixed formulas with standard conversion factors to get kg CO₂e for each category.
The AI never decides your number — the formulas do. Your result is saved with the factor version used (v{factorVersion}), so past results never change.
